# AllDamage

A comprehensive GTA V ScriptHookVDN overhaul mod that introduces realistic player survival mechanics, regional limb damage, vehicle physics, and dynamic world interactions.

## Features

- **In-Game Settings Menu (F10):** Powered by LemonUI. Easily switch difficulty levels anytime (Normal, Hardcore, Simulation).
- **Player Survival & Limb Damage:**
  - **Ragdoll Effect:** Heavy injury forces character ragdoll.
  - **Bleeding Mechanics:** Low health triggers continuous bleeding and pain audio.
  - **Broken Legs:** Prevents sprinting and forces injured walking animations.
  - **Broken Arms:** Causes heavy camera shaking while aiming.
  - **Head Trauma:** Triggers a temporary visual screen effect.
- **Vehicle Physics & Damage:**
  - **Heavy Collisions:** Severe impacts cause tire bursts, wheel detachment, broken doors, and radiator leaks.
  - **Engine Degradation:** Power loss, steering pull, and random stalling at critical engine health.
  - **Radiator Leak System:** Overheats and completely disables the engine over time.
- **World & Environment:**
  - **Weather Dynamics:** Modified vehicle engine power during rainy or stormy conditions in Simulation mode.
  - **Police Calls:** Civilians notice heavily damaged vehicles and report you to the police.

## Requirements

- [ScriptHookV](http://www.dev-c.com/gtav/scripthookv/)
- [ScriptHookVDN](https://github.com/scripthookvdotnet/scripthookvdotnet) (v3.x or higher)
- [LemonUI](https://github.com/LemonUI/LemonUI) (for SHVDN v3)

## Installation

1. Place `AllDamage.dll` and `LemonUI.SHVDN3.dll` into your GTA V `scripts` folder.
2. Press **F10** in-game to open the configuration menu.
